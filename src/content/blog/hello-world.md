## The circle that started it

The book begins with the oldest problem in the subject: find every rational point on the
circle

$$
x^2 + y^2 = 1.
$$

Draw a line through the known point $(-1, 0)$ with rational slope $t$. It meets the circle
in exactly one more point,

$$
\left( \frac{1 - t^2}{1 + t^2},\ \frac{2t}{1 + t^2} \right),
$$

and every rational point arises this way. Clear denominators and you have every
Pythagorean triple. Try the same trick on a cubic curve and it fails. Explaining *why* it
fails takes the whole book.
